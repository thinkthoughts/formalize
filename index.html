01 / Two completed prover milestones

RLT can construct, discover, and independently check proofs.

Automated search is separate from proof authority: a proposed derivation becomes an accepted result only when RLT's kernel replays its certificate against the registered axioms and assumptions.

Bounded proof search and theorem discovery

rlt prove searches for proofs of specified goals. rlt discover enumerates candidate mathematical statements within a declared grammar and bounded search. The generated rlt-proof certificates are replayed by the unchanged proof kernel.

03 / Use the software

Check. Prove. Discover. Explain. Trace. Compose.

Each command has an explicit scope and produces inspectable output. The repository contains reproducible inputs, certificate files, theorem-library entries, and independent checking scripts.

Proof authority

rlt check

Replay each proof step with declared axioms and named assumptions.

Automation

rlt prove · rlt discover

Search bounded goals, enumerate candidates, and create certificates for kernel replay.

Correspondence

rlt explain

Show source excerpts, formal statements, accepted proofs, and separately attributed semantic or physical assessments.

Dependencies

04 / Specification and trust

Four layers. Explicit correspondence.

RLT was designed to preserve distinctions among a written statement, its interpretation, a formally checked relationship, and claims about physical evidence.

01

Written expression

Preserve the exact claim, notation, source, and scope.

02

Convention

Register how notation, definitions, and assumptions are interpreted.

03

Checked relationship

Replay the derivation and record axioms, dependencies, and theorem identity.

04

Physical correspondence

Connect observational claims to measured evidence, provenance, and uncertainty.

A content hash identifies a recorded axiom or source; evidence and interpretation establish the applicable trust in its content. A formal proof establishes the theorem relative to its premises; interpreting it as a claim about the world calls for separate evidence.
