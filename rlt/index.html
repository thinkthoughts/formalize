RLT.

Reading Level Test

We use your theorem prover. You can use ours. RLT is an independently executable proof checker developed from our mathematical specifications, with written expressions, checked relationships, and physical correspondence kept distinct.

01 / Method

Four layers. Explicit correspondence.

RLT makes the writing, its declared interpretation, its verified derivation, and evidence about the world separately inspectable.

01

Written expression

Preserve the symbols written. 1+1 and 2 are different inscriptions.

02

Convention

Name the rule and source used to read notation. Identical symbols can be interpreted differently.

03

Checked relationship

Replay inference steps and retain every axiom and assumption on which the conclusion depends.

04

Physical correspondence

Record observations and supporting evidence separately from mathematical derivations.

A registered axiom remains an assumption. Its SHA-256 hash binds the exact content of the approved file; mathematical truth and physical correspondence have their own justifications.
02 / Reading points

math0 · math5 · math29

These reading points preserve multiple documented meanings. The arithmetic below shows checked relationships attached to the reading levels; their full definitions remain in the source records.

0

math0

Local imagination and counting from zero. Distinguish an expression from an imagined or observed referent.

1 + 1 = 2
5

math5

Local anatomy, counting, and pairing through 5 to 6. Physical quantities require specified units.

5 + 1 = 6
29

math29

Wider mathematical context, pairing through 29 to 30, and separately specified modular relationships.

29 + 1 = 30

These names identify source-defined reading points. Consult the source records for their full meanings.

03 / Verification

Show the derivation.

RLT checks explicit equational derivations, refuses unsupported proof steps, and preserves the assumptions of conditional conclusions. These examples summarize recorded command-line test cases.

goal  5 + 1 = 6
rules inst → cong → trans
ACCEPT · derivation checked
  assumptions: none
  relative to registered axioms

REJECT · 5 + 1 = 6 by calc
  rule 'calc' is unregistered

hypothesis h: 1 + 1 = 3
ACCEPT · conditional on h
REJECT · requested unconditional

What the verdict means

ACCEPT means the checked equation follows from the listed axioms and assumptions. REJECT identifies a derivation the checker refused; the goal remains open to a different proof.
