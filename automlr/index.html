How many attention heads does a Boolean function need?

A paper-to-Lean research record for the exact expressivity of a one-layer, attention-only Transformer. We reproduce the authors' formalization and extend its symmetric-function theory with new kernel-checked corollaries.

The starting point

Viswanathan, Dimitrov, Rocca, and Sirikonda prove a linear-fractional normal form for the model, a threshold-degree lower bound, a strict ten-bit separation, and an exact symmetric-function characterization. The authors released their Lean development and research traces.

The model uses one attention layer, a fixed query token, additive positional embeddings, unrestricted dimensions, and a linear threshold readout. Its head-count conclusions are not claims about all Transformers or trained-model efficiency.

What our fork adds

RPIVerified statement

EXT-002Complementing a symmetric Boolean function preserves head complexity.
EXT-003Exactly n changes iff every adjacent profile value flips.
EXT-004Parity and its complement are the only maximal-head-complexity symmetric Boolean functions.
EXT-005Threshold degree equals profile sign changes; a separate validated patch completes paper Theorem 5.
